candidates (A–F). The human author (Carey James Balboa) approved
**candidate B "alert red balanced"** (RGB 218,55,65) with **body + rings**
recolor as the final OWL-3 CRITICAL standard. Candidate A (this kit's color)
was retained in the study for reference but was not selected.
